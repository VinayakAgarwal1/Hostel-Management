What happens next

The ticket lifecycle from submission to closure.

  1. 01
    Ticket registered

    A unique complaint ID is generated and the status is set to Pending.

  2. 02
    Wing assigned

    The warden routes the ticket to the responsible maintenance wing and technician.

  3. 03
    Work in progress

    Every status change is written to the audit history with a timestamp.

  4. 04
    Resolved & rated

    Once closed, you can rate the resolution and leave feedback for the team.

Data handling

Each submission is written through a single JDBC transaction into complaint and complaint_status_history, using prepared statements throughout.
